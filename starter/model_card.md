# Census Income Prediction Model Card

## Model Details

Model Type: RandomForestClassifier

Framework: Scikit-Learn

## Intended Use

Predict whether annual income exceeds $50K.

## Training Data

UCI Census Income Dataset

## Evaluation Metrics

Precision: 0.735

Recall: 0.638

F1 Score: 0.683

## Ethical Considerations

Predictions may reflect historical societal biases present in the census data, particularly concerning race, gender, education, and occupation.

## Caveats and Recommendations

This model should not be used as the sole basis for employment, lending, or legal decisions.
